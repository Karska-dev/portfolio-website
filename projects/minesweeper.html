How it plays

  1. (1)

    Check a cell

    A checked cell shows how many bombs are around it.

  2. (2)

    Flag the bombs

    Flag the cells you think are hiding one. You can unflag them too.

  3. (3)

    Win or lose

    Check a bomb and you lose. Flag all twenty and you win.
